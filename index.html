❓ How to Play

  1. Slide your finger (or the mouse, or ← →) to move the paddle. Tap to launch a held ball.
  2. Clear every breakable brick to finish a room. Steel never breaks — it is there to get in your way.
  3. A dropped ball costs health, and health carries across the whole climb. Run out and the run is over. With several balls in play a spare one only costs half — it is your last ball that really hurts.
  4. Every room you clear offers a card. Cards last the whole run and stack into builds — Fireball plus Explosive plus Chain Lightning is a different game.
  5. Your deck holds 12 cards. After that, taking one means giving one up.
  6. Pick your route up the map. Elites hit harder and pay better; campfires heal; the boss is at the top of every act.
  7. Armour blunts raw damage. Piercing and Heavy are the answer to it.
  8. Death ends the run — but you keep the shards, and the Archive remembers.

Leave the spire?

Your climb is saved — you can pick it up where you left off.
